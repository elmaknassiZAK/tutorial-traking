## Questions

**1.** Kteb men rassek structure kamla dyal page HTML (doctype, html, head, body).

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>

```html
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="fr">

```

**2.** Chnou l far9 bin `<ul>` w `<ol>` ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>`ul` liste bla tartib (points), `ol` liste mr9ma (1, 2, 3).</details>

**3.** Ila kenti f `blog/post.html`, kifach tktb lien l `index.html` li f racine ?

**4.** 3lach `alt` mohim ? W imta katkhllih khawi ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>Kayt9ra mn lecteurs d'écran, kayban ila l'image ma t7mlatch, w kay3awen Google. Katkhllih khawi (`alt=""`) ila kant l'image ghir décoration.</details>

**5.** Chnou kaydir `target="_blank"` ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>Kay7el l lien f onglet jdid.</details>

**6.** F tableau, chnou `tr`, `th`, `td` ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>`tr` ster, `th` khana dyal titre, `td` khana dyal data.</details>

**7.** Kifach tkhlli `label` mrbout b `input` ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>`<label for="x">` w `<input id="x">` b nefs l valeur.</details>

**8.** Chnou khass f les radios bach t9der tkhtar ghir wa7ed ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>Kolhom khass ykoun 3endhom nefs `name`.</details>

**9.** Chnou l far9 bin `GET` w `POST` ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>`GET` data kayban f URL (recherche, filtres). `POST` data f body dyal requête (login, inscription, data sensible).</details>

**10.** Chnou l far9 bin `section` w `article` ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>`article` contenu mosta9el y9der yt9ra bo7do (post, produit). `section` jiha mn page 3endha titre.</details>

**11.** Ch7al mn `main` w ch7al mn `h1` khass ykoun f page ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>Wa7ed men kol wa7ed.</details>

**12.** 3lach `<div onclick="...">` machi mzyan ?

<details><summary>Jawab</summary>Ma kayt3refch 3lih clavier w lecteurs d'écran. L actions khass `button`.</details>

## 🏆 Défi (ila salit kolchi)

Bni `projects/j5-defi/index.html` : page dyal **recette dyal tajine** fiha :
- `header` b titre w `nav`
- `article` fih image b `figure`/`figcaption`, liste dyal ingrédients (`ul`), étapes (`ol`)
- `table` dyal valeurs nutritionnelles
- `aside` b 3 recettes khrin (liens)
- `form` sghir "3ti rayek" (smiya, note b radio 1–5, commentaire)
- `footer`
